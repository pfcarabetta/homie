Homie Business is built for property managers and short-term rental hosts who handle issues across multiple properties — and don't have time to call around for quotes when something breaks at 9 PM.

## Workspace basics

A **workspace** is the container for your operation:
- One workspace per company (most users)
- Multiple properties live inside it
- Multiple team members can access it (admin / coordinator / read-only)
- All dispatches, vendors, schedules, and bookings are scoped to the workspace

If you manage properties for two unrelated companies, you'd want two workspaces.

## First-time setup checklist

1. **Sign up at homiepro.ai/business** — uses the same login as Homie consumer; we'll auto-create a workspace from your account.
2. **Add properties** in the Properties tab. See [Adding properties](business/adding-properties).
3. **Invite team members** in the Team tab. Roles: Admin (everything), Coordinator (dispatches + bookings), Read-only (view-only).
4. **Set up preferred vendors** in the Vendors tab. See [Preferred vendors](business/preferred-vendors).
5. **Connect Slack** (optional but recommended) for real-time dispatch + booking notifications.

After that, the day-to-day is mostly the Dispatches tab and the Guest Reporter (when guests submit issues).

## Two main workflows

**Reactive — guest or PM-reported issue:**
1. Issue lands in the system (guest form, PM submits, or scheduled task)
2. Auto-categorize + dispatch to preferred vendors
3. Provider quotes flow back
4. PM picks one → booking
5. Provider does the work, PM rates the outcome

**Proactive — scheduled maintenance:**
1. Set up recurring tasks per property (HVAC filter changes, water heater flush, etc.)
2. Tasks fire on schedule + auto-dispatch to preferred vendors
3. Same dispatch / quote / booking flow as reactive
