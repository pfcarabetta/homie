Once you've dispatched to a vendor a few times, **Scorecards** show their performance against your portfolio so you know who's actually pulling their weight.

## What's tracked

Per vendor, across all the dispatches and bookings they've been part of:

| Metric | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Response rate** | % of dispatches they responded to (any way: quote / decline / acknowledge) |
| **Quote rate** | % of dispatches where they actually quoted (vs declined) |
| **Avg response time** | Median time to first response |
| **Avg quote vs others** | Their quote relative to other quotes on the same job (-20% = 20% cheaper than peers) |
| **On-time rate** | % of bookings where they showed up within their stated window |
| **Completion rate** | % of bookings that hit "completed" status without escalation |
| **Avg PM rating** | Your team's 1-5 star rating after each job |

## How the data gets there

- Response data is captured automatically from the dispatch flow
- Quote pricing compared automatically across same-job competitor quotes
- On-time + completion status set by you (or coordinator) from the booking detail
- Ratings prompted in the booking detail after work is marked complete

You don't have to do extra work — most of it lands automatically. The piece that depends on you is **rating the bookings** after they close. Make this a 30-second weekly habit and the scorecards stay accurate.

## Reading the scorecards

The **Vendor Scorecards** tab shows the full table sortable by any column. The dashboard usually surfaces:

- **🏆 Top 5 vendors** — by composite score (response × quote-competitiveness × on-time × rating)
- **⚠️ Bottom 5** — vendors trending down
- **🚀 Rising stars** — recent improvers (last 90 days vs prior 90)
- **❄️ Cooling off** — recent decliners

## Using scorecards in dispatch

You can set a **minimum score threshold** in workspace settings: vendors below the threshold won't be auto-contacted by the dispatch flow even if they're flagged as preferred. They'll still show up in your manual override list — but the auto-cascade skips them.

Useful for letting a slowly-declining vendor "age out" gracefully without you having to manually demote them.

## Sharing the data

You can export scorecards as CSV (Vendors → Scorecards → Export) for owner reports, vendor performance reviews, or your accountant.

## Privacy

Vendors **don't see their own scorecards** in the marketplace network. Each PM's scorecards are private to their workspace. We only use aggregate data (anonymized) to inform Homie's broader marketplace ranking.
