When you've found the quote you want, tap **Accept** on the card. The booking is instant — no extra confirmation, no email tag.

## What happens the moment you accept

1. **The pro gets notified** — SMS + email with your name, phone, address, and a link to the job details.
2. **The booking shows up in your account** under "My Bookings."
3. **You get a confirmation SMS** with the pro's contact info.
4. **The other pros' quotes get marked closed** — they were quoting in good faith, but you've picked.

From there, **you and the pro are connected directly**. They'll usually call or text within an hour to lock in the appointment time.

## How payment works

Homie doesn't take payment for the actual repair. **You pay the pro directly** — usually after the work is done, by whatever method they accept (card, check, Venmo, Zelle). The dispatch fee you paid Homie is the only thing we charge.

This matters: if the price changes once they see the job in person, that's between you two to renegotiate. We're not the middle.

## What if you change your mind

Once accepted, it's a real booking — the pro is now planning their day around it. If you need to cancel:

- **More than 24 hours out**: just let the pro know directly. No drama.
- **Less than 24 hours**: still let them know directly, but expect them to be reasonably annoyed. Some may charge a trip fee if they were already on the way.

You can mark the booking cancelled in your account, which sends them an automated SMS — but a personal text is more polite.

## Tracking the booking

Open **My Bookings** in your account to see:
- Provider contact info (one tap to call or text)
- Quoted price + availability
- Booking confirmation timestamp
- Job details (the original description + your dispatch summary)

If the pro added a private note when they quoted, you'll see it here too.

## Aftercare

After the work is done, you'll get a follow-up email asking how it went. Your rating helps the next homeowner pick the right pro. It also feeds back into Homie's ranking — pros with consistent 5-star outcomes float to the top.
