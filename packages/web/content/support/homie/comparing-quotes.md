Once your job dispatches, quotes start landing in your account in real time. You'll see each one as a card with the provider's name, rating, price, availability, and any notes they sent.

## What's in each quote card

- **Price** — what they'd charge you for the work
- **Provider name + rating** — pulled from their Google or Yelp profile
- **Availability** — when they could come out
- **Channel** — how they responded (phone, SMS, web)
- **Note** — any context they added ("I'd need to see it first to confirm")

## How to read them

Don't just pick the cheapest. The fastest signal is **rating × availability × price**:

- A 4.9-star pro at $400 who can come Thursday is usually a better bet than a 4.2-star pro at $320 who can come "sometime next week."
- "I'd need to see it first" is normal for some categories (electrical, structural) — they'll quote firmly after a free visit.
- A wildly low quote sometimes means the pro misunderstood the scope. Read their note.

## Sorting

Quote cards are sorted **lowest price first** by default. Tap the sort menu to switch to highest rating, soonest availability, or most recent.

## How long quotes stay open

Quotes are valid for **48 hours** by default — most pros let you know if they need that window shorter. After 48 hours we mark them stale. You can ask for fresh quotes by re-dispatching.

## What if a quote looks wrong

If a quote seems way too high or way too low, message the pro directly through the quote card before booking. Most are happy to clarify scope. If they can't or won't, just don't pick them — there's no penalty for skipping a quote.

## What happens when you accept

Tap **Accept** on the quote you want. The pro is notified by SMS and email immediately, gets your contact info, and the booking lands in your account. From there it's between you and them — schedule the appointment, do the work, pay them directly.
