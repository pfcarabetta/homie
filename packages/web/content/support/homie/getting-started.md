Homie is the fastest way to get real quotes from local pros for anything around the house — without calling around or filling out a dozen forms. You describe what's broken, we contact a handful of vetted contractors on your behalf, and you get back actual prices instead of "we'll call you to schedule an estimate."

## What you'll do

1. **Describe the issue** in your own words. Type it, talk to Homie by voice, or even video chat — whatever feels easiest.
2. **Pick a tier** based on how fast you need someone (Standard, Priority, or Emergency).
3. **Pay the dispatch fee** — that's what Homie charges to reach out to pros for you. The pros' quotes are theirs.
4. **Compare the quotes** as they roll in (usually within a few hours).
5. **Accept one** and the booking lands in your account with the provider's contact info.

## What Homie isn't

- We're not the contractor — we don't fix anything ourselves. We connect you with people who do.
- We don't take a cut from the pros. They charge you their own price; we charge you a small dispatch fee.
- We don't lock you in. If none of the quotes feel right, ignore them — no obligation.

## Why this beats calling around

A typical "get three quotes" project takes a homeowner 4–6 hours of phone tag spread over a week. Homie compresses that to ~5 minutes of describing the issue and a few hours of waiting. You're choosing from real numbers, side by side, instead of guessing whether the first guy's ballpark is fair.

## Ready to start?

Just hit the homepage, type what's going on, and we'll take it from there. Most folks have quotes back within 2–4 hours.
