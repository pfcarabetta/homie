When you submit a job, Homie reaches out to a handful of local pros on your behalf — by phone, SMS, and email simultaneously — and asks them to quote your specific repair. You don't talk to anyone until you've got real numbers in hand.

## The order of operations

1. **You submit** — describe the issue, pick a tier, pay the dispatch fee.
2. **We pick the pros** — vetted local contractors in your zip code who handle that category, ranked by rating, response history, and proximity.
3. **We reach out** — Homie's AI calls them with your description, texts them a link, and emails the request. Each one can respond at their own speed.
4. **They quote you** — directly, in their own words, with their own price. We pass each quote back to you in your account in real time.
5. **You pick one** (or none).

## How many pros we contact

| Tier | Initial wave | Follow-up if no responses |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | 5 | +5 after 30 min, up to 3 waves |
| Priority | 10 | +5 after 30 min, up to 3 waves |
| Emergency | 15 | +5 after 30 min, up to 3 waves |

If a pro already responded with a quote, we never re-contact them in a later wave for the same job.

## How long it takes

| Tier | Typical first quote | Most quotes by |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | 1–4 hours | end of day |
| Priority | 30 min – 2 hours | a few hours |
| Emergency | 5–30 min | within an hour |

If you're in a rural area or it's late at night, expect the longer end of those ranges.

## What "vetted" means

Every pro Homie reaches out to has been screened on:
- Real Google or Yelp profile with reviews
- Service area covers your zip
- The category matches what you need

We don't reach out to randoms off Craigslist.

## What happens if nobody quotes

Rare — but if it happens, we automatically expand outreach to a wider radius and slightly lower rating threshold. You don't have to do anything. If after three expansion waves nobody bites (~90 minutes for Priority), we'll let you know and refund the dispatch fee.
