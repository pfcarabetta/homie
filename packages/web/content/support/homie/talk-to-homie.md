Sometimes typing is the slow way. Homie has two hands-free modes for describing what's broken — both feed the same diagnosis pipeline that produces your dispatch summary.

## Talk to Homie (voice)

Tap **Talk to Homie** on the homepage or in the chat. We'll request mic access and Homie's AI will ask follow-up questions out loud. You answer back like a phone call. Homie picks up on details you wouldn't have thought to type — model numbers, when it started, what you've already tried.

- **Hands-free mode** (default): Homie auto-listens, detects when you stop talking, and replies. No buttons.
- **Tap-to-record mode**: hit a button to start, hit it again to stop. Better in noisy rooms.

Switch between them with the toggle inside the voice panel.

## Video chat with Homie

Tap **Video chat with Homie**. Same as voice, but you can also point your camera at the issue while you describe it. Homie reads the video — sees the dripping pipe, the cracked tile, the dead outlet — and bakes that into the dispatch summary so the pros know exactly what they're looking at.

This is especially handy for:
- "I don't know what to call this part" — show it
- Brand/model labels — point the camera at the sticker, Homie reads it
- Anything visual where words fall short

## What gets sent to the pros

After the voice or video conversation wraps, Homie generates a clean text summary of the issue and a category. That's what goes out to the pros — they don't see the raw transcript or video. You'll see the summary on the screen before dispatch fires; you can tweak it if you want.

## Privacy

- Voice + video are processed for the live conversation only. Audio isn't stored once the conversation ends; the dispatch summary is what persists.
- Pros never see or hear your voice or video. They get the AI-generated text summary.

## If voice/video doesn't work

- iOS sometimes blocks audio playback if the page hasn't received a tap first. The buttons on the homepage handle this; if you got to /quote some other way, tap "Talk to Homie" once to prime audio.
- Mic permission denied? Reload and accept when prompted, or fall back to typing.
- Slow connection? Voice + video will degrade gracefully — typing always works as a fallback.
