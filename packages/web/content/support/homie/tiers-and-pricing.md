Homie charges a one-time dispatch fee per job. The fee covers reaching out to pros on your behalf — the contractors quote their own prices separately. The tier you pick controls how many pros we contact and how aggressively we follow up.

## The three tiers

| | Standard | Priority | Emergency |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Initial pros contacted** | 5 | 10 | 15 |
| **First quote, typical** | 1–4 hours | 30 min – 2 hours | 5–30 min |
| **Best for** | Cosmetic, planned, "this week" | "Today or tomorrow" | "It's flooding" |

## When to pick which

- **Standard** — most things. Replacing a faucet, painting a room, fence repair, anything you can wait a day or two on.
- **Priority** — same-day or next-day matters. AC stopped working in summer, bathroom only works one toilet, garage door won't open. You're willing to pay a bit more to get there sooner.
- **Emergency** — water actively coming in, gas smell, no heat below freezing, locked out. We hit 15 pros in parallel and the first one to confirm wins.

## What the dispatch fee gets you

- Outreach to the tier's number of pros (with up to 3 expansion waves if needed)
- AI-generated job descriptions sent to each pro so they don't have to ask 20 questions back
- Real-time tracking of who's responding and what they're quoting
- A consolidated view comparing quotes side by side
- A refund if nobody bites after 3 expansion waves

## What the dispatch fee doesn't cover

- The repair itself. The pro you pick charges you their own price.
- Travel/diagnostic fees if a pro needs to come out before quoting (rare — most quote remotely from your description and photos).

## The contractor's price is between you and them

Homie doesn't take a cut from the pro. They quote their actual price and you pay them directly when the work is done. The dispatch fee is the only thing Homie charges.
