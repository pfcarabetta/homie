Most jobs sail through. Here's what to do when they don't.

## Nobody has quoted me

If you're past the typical window (4 hours for Standard, 2 for Priority, 30 min for Emergency) with zero quotes:

1. **Check that your description is specific.** "Leak" is too vague — pros need to know what's leaking, where, and how bad. You can edit the dispatch summary and resend.
2. **We auto-expand outreach** to a wider radius and slightly lower rating threshold every 30 minutes, up to 3 waves. So even if the first batch ignored you, more pros will see it.
3. **After 3 waves** (~90 minutes), if nobody bites, we automatically refund the dispatch fee. You'll get an email.

## A pro quoted but went silent after I accepted

Try the obvious first: call or text the number on the booking card. Pros sometimes get slammed and forget to confirm.

If 24 hours pass with no contact:
1. Mark the booking as "no contact" in **My Bookings**.
2. Re-dispatch the same job. The dispatch fee is waived in this case — write to yo@homiepro.ai with the job ID and we'll credit you.

## The pro showed up and the price was way higher

This is between you and the pro to negotiate. A quote is a good-faith estimate, not a binding contract — sometimes scope shifts when they see the actual job.

That said: if the new price is wildly different from the quote (2×+) and you don't agree, you're free to send them away and pick a different quote. Most pros won't charge a trip fee in that case if you were polite about it.

If the pro behaved unprofessionally, please tell us at yo@homiepro.ai with the job ID. Repeat issues get pros removed from the network.

## I want a refund of the dispatch fee

We refund the dispatch fee in three cases, automatically:

1. **No quotes after 3 expansion waves** — auto-refund within 24 hours
2. **Stripe payment failed but we accidentally dispatched** — auto-refund
3. **Duplicate dispatch** (we accidentally hit your job twice) — auto-refund

For other cases (changed your mind, found a pro elsewhere, etc.), email yo@homiepro.ai within 7 days of the dispatch and we'll review case-by-case. Generally, we'll refund if you didn't accept any of the quotes.

## My job dispatched twice / something got duplicated

Real bug, sorry. Email yo@homiepro.ai with the job ID(s). We'll refund the duplicate fee and investigate.

## I can't log in / forgot my password

Reset link on the **Log in** page. Check your spam folder. If still stuck, email yo@homiepro.ai from the address on your account.
