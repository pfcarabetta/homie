The AI Deep Dive is the unfair advantage that makes Homie Inspect actually useful. Click any item in your report and you get a friendly, plain-English breakdown of what's going on — written like a contractor friend explaining it to you over coffee, not like a manual.

## What you get on every item

When you tap **AI Deep Dive** on an item, the AI immediately answers five questions in 150 words or less:

1. **What is this?** — explained in normal English, no jargon
2. **Why it matters** — the actual consequence of leaving it
3. **If ignored** — what happens in 6 months, 2 years, 5 years
4. **DIY or pro?** — honest assessment of whether this is fixable yourself
5. **Cost context** — typical price ranges and what drives them up or down

That's the auto-generated summary. From there you can keep asking follow-up questions — "what brand of replacement should I get?", "is this worth pushing back on the seller?", "can I just live with it for 6 months?" — and the AI answers in context.

## What the AI knows

For each item, it has:
- The inspector's exact words
- The severity, category, and location
- The cost estimate range
- Photo descriptions (what the inspector noted in their photos)
- The full inspection report's other items (so it can spot related issues)

It does **not** make up brand names, specific models, or pricing it isn't sure about. If you ask "what brand should I buy?" it'll give you the category of product to search for and what spec matters, not a fake SKU.

## The "Try DIY" button

When the heuristic thinks an item might be safe to DIY (right severity, right category, reasonable cost), you'll see a green **🔧 DIY** badge on it and a **"Try DIY"** button below the AI Deep Dive. Tapping it generates:

- 5–10 step instructions specific to your item
- A parts list with Amazon search links
- Cost estimate (DIY supply cost vs typical pro cost — see the savings)
- Safety warnings + when to stop and call a pro

Some items have a hard "no" from the AI (gas, panel work, structural) — for those, the DIY badge never appears. The AI will tell you why and what kind of pro to call.

## Privacy

Your conversations with the AI are private to your account. Your inspector doesn't see them. Other homeowners don't see them. We don't use your conversations to train models.
