When you accept a quote on the Quotes tab, it shows up in **Bookings** with the provider's contact info, quoted price, availability, and a deep link back to the inspection report it came from.

## What's in each booking card

Tap a card to expand it:

- **Provider name + rating + review count**
- **Quoted price** (and availability if they shared one)
- **Service address** — the property the inspection was for
- **Booked timestamp** + status (Confirmed / Completed / Cancelled)
- **Provider's note** if they sent one ("I'd want to see it first to confirm scope")
- **One-tap call** + **email** buttons to reach the pro
- **Inspect badge** — visual reminder this came from an inspection (vs a /quote dispatch)

## Filtering

The filter row at the top lets you scope by:
- **Status** — Confirmed / Completed / Cancelled
- **Category** — plumbing / electrical / HVAC / etc.
- **Property** — when you have multiple inspections (rename them to make this easier; see [Renaming reports](inspect/renaming-reports))
- **Date range** — when bookings were confirmed

## How payment works

Homie doesn't charge for the repair itself. **You pay the pro directly** — when they finish the work, by whatever method they accept. The booking just connects you and tracks the relationship.

## After the appointment

Bookings auto-flip to **Completed** 14 days after they were confirmed. If something goes sideways before then (you cancel, the pro no-shows), tell us at yo@homiepro.ai and we'll mark it cancelled.

## Cancellation

If you need to cancel:
- **24 hours+ out**: text or call the pro directly. They'll appreciate the heads-up.
- **Less than 24 hours**: still call them. They may charge a trip fee if they were already on the way.

We don't have a one-tap cancel button on the homeowner side yet (coming soon). For now, message the pro and email yo@homiepro.ai with the booking ID — we'll update the status.

## Why bookings show even on Essential tier? They don't

The Bookings tab is gated to Professional and Premium because the Quotes tab is. If you only have an Essential report, you'll see an upgrade prompt instead.
