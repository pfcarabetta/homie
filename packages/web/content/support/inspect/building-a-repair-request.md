A repair request is a one-page PDF you (or your agent) hand to the seller's side asking them to credit or fix specific items from your inspection. Homie builds it for you in two minutes from the items + quotes you've already collected. This is the killer feature for buyers.

> **Premium feature.** The Negotiations tab unlocks on the Premium tier. See [Pricing tiers](inspect/pricing-tiers).

## How it works

1. Open the **Negotiations** tab in your Inspect portal.
2. Check the box on every item you want the seller to address.
3. For each item, pick the dollar amount you're asking for using the source dropdown:
   - **Provider quote** (most leverage — uses a real contractor's price)
   - **AI estimate** (when you don't have a quote)
   - **Custom amount** (your own number — type it in)
4. Click **Download repair request PDF** at the top right.

## What's in the PDF

- Property address + your name + inspection date + request date
- A line for each selected item with the severity flag, location, description, and the dollar amount you're asking for
- A grand total at the bottom
- A signature line for you and a date line
- Homie footer (small, polite — your agent won't be embarrassed by it)

## Tips for maximum leverage

- **Real provider quotes carry more weight than AI estimates.** Dispatch big items first on the Quotes tab so you have actual numbers when you build the request.
- **Don't include every monitor-level item.** Focus on safety hazards and recommended fixes that matter to you. Including 30 cosmetic items waters down the credibility of the request.
- **Ask for a credit, not a fix.** Sellers usually prefer paying out a credit at closing over scheduling repairs themselves. Credits also let you pick the contractor.
- **Bundle related items into one ask.** "All plumbing items: $1,200" reads cleaner than 5 separate plumbing line items.

## What sellers typically agree to

In most contingency-period negotiations:
- **Safety hazards** → near-100% addressed (legal liability if they're in disclosure)
- **Urgent items** → mostly addressed, sometimes with a credit instead of a fix
- **Recommended items** → 50/50, depends on the strength of your other leverage
- **Monitor items** → rarely addressed, sometimes met with a "we'll disclose"

Buyers using Negotiations recover 3–5× their report cost in seller credits, on average.

## Updating after seller responds

The Negotiations tab also tracks what the seller agreed to and what credits actually closed:

- **Seller agreed amount** — type in what they said yes to per item
- **Status** — pending / agreed / credited / escrow holdback / dropped
- **Credit issued** — final amount you actually got at closing

This gives you a running tally so you know exactly where the negotiation stands without spreadsheet juggling.
