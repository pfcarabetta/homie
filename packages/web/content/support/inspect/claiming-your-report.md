When your inspector finishes parsing and sends you the report, you'll get an email with a "View your report" link. That link drops you straight into your portal — no password, no signup wall.

## What the link does

The link contains a one-time claim token. The first time you click it:

1. Homie verifies the token is valid and tied to your email.
2. We create (or link to) your homeowner account using that email.
3. You land in the **My Reports** tab with the report open.

After that first click, you're logged in. Subsequent visits to homiepro.ai/inspect-portal go straight to your account.

## If you didn't get the email

Check spam, then check the email address your inspector had on file (sometimes it's the agent's address, not yours). If still nothing, ask your inspector to resend — they have a "Send copy" button on their end that costs them nothing.

## If the link expired

Claim links are valid for 90 days. If you click an expired one, we'll show a friendly "ask your inspector to resend" message. They'll see your request in their dashboard and can fire a new link.

## If you already have a Homie account

Same email = same account. The report just gets added to your existing **My Reports** tab. Nothing else changes.

## Multiple reports? No problem

If you're shopping for a house, you'll often have inspections on multiple properties. Each one gets its own report card on the **My Reports** tab. You can:

- **Rename them** ("Dream home", "Backup option") so they're easier to spot. Click the pencil icon on the card.
- **Filter the cross-property views** (Items tab, Bookings tab, Negotiations tab) to a single property using the property dropdown at the top of each tab.

## Privacy

Your inspector sees: the property address, your name, your email, and that you opened the report. They don't see what you do inside the portal — your AI Deep Dives, your repair request, your DIY clicks, your notes — that's all yours.
