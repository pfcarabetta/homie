A house transaction generates more than just an inspection report. Pest reports, seller disclosures, sewer scopes, roof inspections, mold reports, foundation reports — they all carry information that, when read alongside the inspection, often produces a "wait, that explains it" moment.

The **Documents** tab lets you drop those in alongside your inspection. Homie parses each one and **cross-references** them with the inspection — surfacing contradictions and corroborations the human eye might miss.

## What you can upload

We currently parse 11 document types:
- Pest report (termite, WDO)
- Seller disclosure
- Sewer scope
- Roof inspection
- Foundation report
- HVAC inspection
- Electrical inspection
- Septic inspection
- Mold inspection
- Pool / spa inspection
- Chimney inspection

Each gets parsed into a structured summary — findings, severity, recommended actions, sourced page numbers from the original PDF.

## How cross-reference works

After you upload a supporting doc, Homie's AI scans it against your inspection items and flags **correlations**:

> 💡 **Inspection** found "active moisture in basement, NE corner" + **seller disclosure** notes "2022 leak in basement (repaired)" + **pest report** flags "wood-destroying organism activity, NE basement perimeter"

That kind of three-way correlation is the smoking gun your agent dreams about during negotiation. It also catches the opposite — where the seller's disclosure contradicts what the inspector found.

## Where the cross-references show up

- **Items tab** — items that have correlated content from supporting docs get a 🔗 **Cross-referenced** badge. Filter to just those for negotiation prep.
- **Documents tab** — each supporting doc shows the items it correlates with, in both directions.
- **AI Deep Dive** — when you open the deep dive on a cross-referenced item, the AI references the supporting doc's findings in its analysis.

## When to upload supporting docs

Buyers usually have these floating around:
- The pest report (often required by lender)
- The seller disclosure (every state requires one)
- A sewer scope (recommended on homes 30+ years old)
- A specialist report your inspector recommended

Drop them all in. Each takes ~60 seconds to parse. Cross-references regenerate automatically as you add docs.

## Inspector-bundled documents

If your inspector bundled supporting docs at upload time, they're already in your portal — no action needed. You'll see them in **Documents** when you arrive, already parsed.
