The Homie inspector partner program is the simplest add-on we could design: you sell our report add-on at every inspection, you charge your client retail ($99–$299), you pay us wholesale ($49–$99), you keep the spread ($50–$200) per report. No subscription, no monthly minimums, no platform fee. Free to join, you only pay when you upload a report you've already sold.

## What you're actually selling

The Homie report add-on transforms your static PDF into an interactive digital deliverable for your client:
- Every item in your report parsed and triaged by AI (severity, category, location)
- AI Deep Dive on every item (your client can ask follow-up questions)
- DIY guides for safe items (with Amazon parts lists)
- Real contractor quotes for the bigger stuff (Pro+ tier)
- Repair-request PDF for negotiations (Premium tier)
- Cross-document analysis with pest reports + seller disclosures

Your clients don't get a 60-page PDF and good luck. They get a tool that pays them back during the negotiation and after closing.

## Three tiers, three ways to win

| | Sell at | Pay us | You keep | Margin |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Essential** | $99 | $49 | **$50** | 50% |
| **Professional** | $199 | $79 | **$120** | 60% |
| **Premium** | $299 | $99 | **$200** | 67% |

Most inspectors default to **Professional** ($120 spread) — it's the sweet spot for typical residential buyers. Push **Premium** when the client's deal has high stakes (multi-million dollar buy, complex property, investor with multiple inspections).

## Typical earnings

| Inspections / month | Realistic mix | Net to you |
|---|---|---|
| 8 | 5 Pro + 2 Essential + 1 Premium | ~$900 |
| 12 (typical busy inspector) | 5 Essential + 5 Pro + 2 Premium | **$1,250** |
| 20 | 8 Pro + 6 Premium + 6 Essential | $2,560 |

That's $15K–$30K of pure-margin annual income for an inspector running a normal book of business. No extra time on each job — you upload the report you already wrote.

## How you sell it

1. **Mention it during the inspection walkthrough.** "I include a digital action plan with my report — interactive items, real quotes, cost estimates. Three tiers depending on how much you want to do with it. Most clients pick Professional at $199."
2. **Offer it on the spot** before they sign the inspection agreement, or in your follow-up email with the PDF.
3. **Upload through your inspector portal** — pay wholesale, we email the parsed report to your client automatically.

The hardest part is the first 5 sales — the script-feel goes away after that.

## Free to join, pay only when you sell

There's no signup fee. There's no monthly minimum. The wholesale cost is what you pay Homie *after* your client has already paid you retail. So it's literally impossible to lose money on a sale — the cash flows in your favor by definition.
