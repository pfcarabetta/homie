Past closing, you're not done with your inspection report. The **Maintenance** tab takes the items your inspector flagged "monitor" and combines them with a season-by-season home maintenance checklist tailored to your property — so the small things don't become $5,000 surprises in 18 months.

> **Premium feature.** See [Pricing tiers](inspect/pricing-tiers).

## What's in there

**Monitor-flagged items** from your inspection — the ones that aren't problems yet but should be watched. Each one shows up with:
- The original inspector finding
- A "next check" suggestion (3 months / 6 months / annual)
- A note field for your own observations
- A "Mark complete" button when you've handled it

**Seasonal tasks** — generic but tailored to your property's region, age, and systems:
- HVAC filter changes
- Water heater flush (annual)
- Gutter cleaning (twice yearly)
- Smoke detector tests
- Disposal cleaning
- Outdoor drain inspections
- Pre-winter prep / post-winter checks

## Why it matters more than it sounds

Most expensive home repairs are inevitable but predictable. The water heater that floods the basement was making sediment noise for 18 months. The HVAC compressor that dies in July was running rough all spring. The gutter overflow that rotted the fascia could've been a $40 cleaning.

Every monitor item your inspector flagged is a future repair — the maintenance plan gives you the calendar to actually catch them before they escalate.

## Notifications

You can opt into reminder emails per task:
- 7 days before the next due date
- On the due date if you didn't snooze it

Manage these in **Settings → Notifications**.

## Marking items done

Tap **Mark complete** when you've handled a task. Completed items grey out and skip to the bottom; the next-due-date resets based on the cadence (annual tasks come back in 12 months, etc.). Your full history is saved so you can see "last flushed: October 2025" the next time the question comes up.

## Sharing with a spouse

If you and your spouse both have access to the same homeowner account (same login), you'll both see the same maintenance state. We don't have multi-user accounts on Inspect yet — for now, share the login.
