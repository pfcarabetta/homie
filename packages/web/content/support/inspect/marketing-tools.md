You don't have to be a marketer to sell the Homie add-on. We give you ready-made copy, a personal partner page URL, and a few tactical tools to make the conversion happen during or right after your inspection.

## Your partner page

Every inspector gets a public partner page at `homiepro.ai/inspect/inspectors/<your-slug>` — yours to share with clients before, during, or after the inspection. It explains what they're getting (without inspector-only inside-baseball), the three tiers, and includes a "powered by your inspector" line.

Find your link in **Marketing → Partner page** in your inspector portal. Copy it, paste it in:
- Your email signature
- Inspection contract / agreement
- Follow-up email template
- Text after the walkthrough

## Pre-built scripts that actually work

In **Marketing → Sales scripts**, we've got short scripts for:
- 30-second walkthrough mention
- Email pitch (post-inspection)
- "What's in the digital report?" objection handling
- Up-sell from Essential to Pro / Premium

They're written in your voice, not corporate. Use them verbatim or adapt.

## Email signature snippet

Copy/paste into Gmail signature settings:

> 📋 Get the digital action plan for your inspection report — interactive items, real contractor quotes, repair-request PDF for negotiations. **Tap here →** *[your partner link]*

Cleaner than a marketing pitch, and most of your existing clients will click out of curiosity.

## QR code

In **Marketing → QR code**, we generate a printable PDF with your partner link encoded. Print it, slip it into your inspection report binder, hand it to the client during the walkthrough. Some inspectors put one on the back of their business card.

## Inspection-day script (the high-converting one)

Most inspectors who hit 70%+ attach rate use a variant of this:

> "I'll have your report to you tomorrow morning. I also offer a digital version through Homie — same items but interactive, with real contractor quotes if you want them, and a one-page PDF you can hand your agent for negotiations. Three tiers, $99 to $299. Most of my clients pick the middle one. Want me to add it?"

That's it. 25 seconds. The "most of my clients pick the middle one" anchor pushes Pro by default, which is the $120-spread tier.

## Conversion tracking

Your **Earnings** dashboard shows:
- Reports uploaded per tier per month
- Inspector earnings (your spread × volume)
- Conversion rate (% of inspections where you sold the add-on)

The conversion-rate number is the one to watch — successful partners run 50–80%. If you're under 30%, the script + partner-link visibility usually fixes it.
