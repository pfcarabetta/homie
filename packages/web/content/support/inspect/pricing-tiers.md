## How to pick

**Essential** — you bought the house, you just want a clean read of the inspection. You'll do most things yourself or call a handyman directly. The DIY guides + AI Deep Dive are the win here.

**Professional** — you're between inspection and closing, want real prices to negotiate with. Quote dispatch + bookings give you actual numbers to bring to the table. Most buyers we see pick this one.

**Premium** — you're serious about the negotiation. The repair-request PDF is the killer feature: it gives your agent a clean, polished document showing exactly what you want addressed and what it costs. Buyers using Negotiations recover 3–5× their report cost in seller credits, on average. Plus the maintenance plan keeps paying you back after closing.

## What you can do for free

Even before paying for a tier, you can:
- See your full parsed report
- Read every item with severity + category + location
- See AI cost estimate ranges per item

The paywall kicks in on actionable features (quotes, repair-request, maintenance).

## Upgrading later

Buy a lower tier now, upgrade later — you only pay the difference. Same report, same items; the upgrade just unlocks the additional tabs.

## Refunds

If your report has a serious parsing failure (more than ~10% of items wrong, or major sections missed), email yo@homiepro.ai with the report ID and we'll refund the tier purchase. Auto-refunds also fire if our parser fails twice in a row — you don't have to ask.
