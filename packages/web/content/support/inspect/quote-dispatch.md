The Items tab on Professional and Premium tiers lets you select inspection items and dispatch them to local contractors for real quotes. Not estimates — real prices from real pros, usually within a few hours.

## Pick the items you want priced

In the **Items** tab, every item has a checkbox. Tick the ones you want quotes for — usually the bigger-ticket items where a $300 estimate range is too vague to negotiate from. Items grouped by category will dispatch to a single category-matched pro batch (so you get one quote covering all the plumbing items, not five separate plumbing quotes).

## Hit "Get Quotes for X items"

The button at the top of the Items tab fires the dispatch. You'll see:

- Your selected items grouped by category
- A confirmation of which categories will go out (plumbing, electrical, HVAC, etc.)
- Cost estimate ranges as a baseline

Once dispatched, the **Quotes** tab fills in over the next few hours as pros respond.

## How long it takes

| Severity / scope | Typical first quote | Most quotes |
|---|---|---|
| Single item, small | 1–4 hours | end of day |
| Multi-item bundle | 2–6 hours | next day |
| Big-ticket (roof, foundation) | next day | within 2 days |

Pros sometimes need to come out for the larger jobs, in which case you'll see "I'd need to see it first" notes on their quotes. Free site visits are common; they'll quote firmly after.

## Reading quotes

Each quote card shows:
- Provider name + Google/Yelp rating
- Total quoted price
- Availability ("can come Thursday")
- Per-item breakdown if it's a bundle, or "covers items 1, 3, 5"
- Optional note from the pro

Sort by lowest price, highest rating, or soonest availability — your call.

## Bundle pricing

Some pros will quote a **bundle price** — a single number to handle multiple items at once, often cheaper than the items individually. You'll see "Bundle: $1,400 (covers 4 items)" on those cards. They're often the best deal if the items are in the same category.

## What dispatch costs

Quote dispatch is included in your Professional or Premium tier — no per-job fee. Dispatch as many bundles as you want; you only pay the pros if you accept their quotes (and they bill you directly, see below).

## When you accept a quote

The booking lands in your **Bookings** tab. From there it's between you and the pro to schedule and pay. Homie isn't in the middle of the actual repair payment.
