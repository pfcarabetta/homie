Home inspections produce 60-page PDFs full of "we recommend addressing this" with no idea of what it'll cost or how serious it is. Homie Inspect parses that PDF with AI and turns it into something you can actually use — a triaged, prioritized list of items with cost estimates, real contractor quotes, DIY options, and a polished repair-request PDF for the seller.

It works whether you're **buying** (use it during the inspection contingency window to negotiate seller credits) or **selling** (use it pre-listing to fix what matters and disclose what doesn't).

## What you get

- **AI-parsed report** — every item your inspector found, organized by severity (safety hazard / urgent / recommended / monitor / informational), category (plumbing / electrical / HVAC / etc.), and location.
- **AI Deep Dive** on any item — what it actually means, what it'll cost, DIY vs pro, what to ask the seller. Like a contractor friend on call, for every item.
- **Real quotes** from local pros (Pro tier+) — not estimates, real prices.
- **Repair-request PDF** (Premium) — built from the items you select with the dollar amounts you're asking for. Hand it to your agent and you've got leverage.
- **Maintenance plan** (Premium) — items flagged "monitor" plus a season-by-season checklist tailored to your property.
- **Cross-document analysis** — drop in pest reports, seller disclosures, sewer scopes; we cross-reference them with the inspection so contradictions stand out.
- **DIY guides + parts lists** — for items that are safe for a confident beginner.

## How you got here

You probably arrived via one of three paths:

1. **Your inspector emailed you a link** — they offered the Homie report add-on at your inspection. Just click the link in the email; no signup required.
2. **You uploaded your own report** at homiepro.ai/inspect — works without an inspector partner, just drop the PDF.
3. **You were referred** — same flow as either of the above, just start at the homepage.
